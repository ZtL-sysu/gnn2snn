## Interpretation note

The mechanism table reports Spearman associations across validation graphs. Teacher margin is included as a simple confound/context diagnostic because low-margin graphs are more likely to flip and may also show larger reconstruction errors. These marginal correlations are descriptive; they are not adjusted causal effects and do not account for teacher-seed uncertainty.
