The empirical study tests whether critical-site sensitivity yields realizable graph-aware conversion gains. Strong frozen-teacher baselines and matched local/non-graph/permuted controls are available. Recent comparisons use teacher41 and512 historically exposed validation graphs per task. Actual-message allocation improves immediate message reconstruction but fails the joint cross-task classification, structure-control and T2 criteria. Historical three-teacher full endpoints are descriptive context, not independent new-method confirmation. The manuscript should distinguish sensitivity, realizability and inference cost without claiming algorithm superiority.
