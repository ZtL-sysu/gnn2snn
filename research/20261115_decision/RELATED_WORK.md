## Cost conventions

Same eight geometric magnitudes plus zero at T1, signed differential corrections later; same thresholds and 693 learned FP32 offsets. Our objective arms add no topology-conditioned online module, no teacher inference, no weight retraining, and no new event type. Parameters occupy 2772 bytes excluding shared thresholds/weights/states. Count actual per-site nonzero scalar events at every tick; residual, readout and dense graph arithmetic are **not** included in this event counter and are not zero cost. Sum amplitudes separately. Instrumented GPU evaluation time includes diagnostics and offline teacher evaluation, so it is not deployed latency or chip energy. No hardware saving claim.

Graph-specific innovation remains unestablished: no accepted structure-dependent encoding has passed the objective gate, so the conditional four-arm structure/label/capacity comparison and external confirmation were not launched. Historical structure controls remain negative evidence with their original bounds.
