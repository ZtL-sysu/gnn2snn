# Complete-data, full-teacher conversion protocol v1

Scope: the user's principal MNIST/CIFAR10 superpixel graph-classification track. Use the completed best-validation full-data four-layer GCN checkpoint from `teacher-full-protocol-v1.md`. Preserve the earlier small-teacher track separately. No primary teacher is narrowed, masked, or trained on a subset.

After the teacher training writes its terminal `done.json`, freeze and hash the best-validation checkpoint. Calibrate six ReLU sites (four residual GCN branches and two MLP hidden activations) with two full passes over **every official training graph**, in original training-split order. First find exact per-channel maxima; then count every activation into 512 bins per channel and 512 global bins per layer. Compute approximate upper-bin 99.9th-percentile thresholds, with a 1e-6 floor for inactive channels. Both global-per-layer and per-channel thresholds are derived from this same full training pass, without labels. Record checkpoint/data/code hashes, graph/activation counts, bin width and thresholds. Histogram binning, BN folding, and training-split calibration are explicit method details.

Evaluate the frozen staged-count forward on **all official validation graphs** for T in {1,2,4,8,16,32}, separately for global and per-channel thresholds. Record teacher and converted top-1, same-example prediction agreement, logit error, per-layer spike totals and exact graph count. Validation can guide later working-point selection, but all attempted thresholds/timesteps and their outcomes are retained. Compare the same theta-scaled output readout across methods. Staged conversion serializes six activation sites and cannot claim an end-to-end T-step latency; residual/input paths may remain real-valued. SynOps is a proxy until a full operator accounting and event implementation are established.

Existing official MNIST/CIFAR10 test examples were used in prior project experiments, so do not describe them as never seen. After the full teacher and conversion decision are frozen, evaluate the entire official test set with the same configuration and record the historical adaptive-exposure limitation. This is not an independent external confirmation. Additional strong baselines, multiple seeds, a more powerful CIFAR teacher, event-level correctness, timing and CCFA review remain required. Do not treat this first diagnostic protocol as a complete CVPR result.
