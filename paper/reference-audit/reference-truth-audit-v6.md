## Triage

Inventory totals across all 42 keys: **16 verified, 21 metadatawrong, 5 unresolved** (`knyazev2019`, `bojkovic2024ot`, `yang2025csqcfs`, `zhou2025edge`, `ji2026gtdl`). Twenty-five rows received fresh verification or context review for this v6 pass; the eight earlier findings are retained from v5 without redoing their searches. `metadatawrong` includes wrong entry type as well as wrong/incomplete author or title fields. Some entries marked metadatawrong are real and citable after fixing their records; several have shortened author lists rather than false underlying works. “Verified” here means bibliographic identity was matched to an authoritative primary record, not that every proposition in the draft is supported by the cited work.

Material context mismatches requiring manuscript review: (1) membrane decoder claim attached to `dualphase2024`; (2) exact optimal-transport description attached to unresolved `bojkovic2024ot`; (3) claim SGNNBench has no conversion method; (4) PASCAL's alleged inhibitory-spike exact-count mechanism; (5) novelty sentence that no published GCN conversion exists, given One-Spike's primary report of GCN conversion. These should be rewritten or checked against the linked full papers before a new submission.

A formatting/count gate cannot establish these records' existence, canonical metadata, or relevance. See [references-corrections-v6.bib](references-corrections-v6.bib) for selected correction candidates. No original bibliography, TeX, code, dataset or run artifact was changed in this audit.
