# Final scientific and policy review

## Scientific disposition

The manuscript is a mechanism study, not a new conversion method. Its contribution rests on controlled hard-event disagreement and exact local temporal equivalence inside graph networks. It attributes DCGS, FTBC, calibration, One-Spike and temporal misalignment to prior work. The norm/margin and affine identities are not claimed as novel theorems. The main unresolved scientific risks are limited independent replication, discovery novelty relative to general quantization/temporal misalignment, and absent modulewise mediation. These are disclosed in the paper and Chinese report; checks do not convert them into positive findings.

Primary-agent review checked event state updates, negative eligibility, weighted decoder, all-competitor margin, first-tick versus cumulative MSE, mean versus sample SD, different fitting horizons, historical exposure, centered train-only scope, Gated analog arithmetic and separate timing boundaries. An independent Luna evidence audit supplied the early and visual source checks before drafting. A later requested independent manuscript review failed due to worker quota; final manuscript review was performed by the primary agent, not described as completed independent review.
