 504   5. Discussion and Conclusion
 505   The experiments expose two scoped information losses in
 506   reconstruction-based evaluation. The measured message
 507   norm omits the signed direction of an actual event error
 508   relative to competing classes, while the measured termi-
 509   nal reconstruction omits the temporal states traversed by
 510   downstream units. Position recovery locates influential sites;
 511   hard-event changes show that improving a local metric can
 512   worsen the decision; exact codeword exchanges demonstrate
 513   that eliminating local terminal error still leaves downstream
 514   temporal ambiguity.
 515      A useful design criterion follows from these distinctions.
 516   Specify the downstream quantity preserved by an approxi-
 517   mation, then evaluate a realizable finite-event change under
 518   fixed task computation. Measure teacher agreement and ac-
 519   curacy separately, and check every required prefix alongside
 520   total activity. Residual centering preserves an affine repre-
 521   sentation identity and reduces a projection proxy, whereas
 522   task-aware calibration changes decisions without a consis-
 523   tent advantage in the evaluated settings. Each intervention is
 524   informative at the level its evidence supports.
 525      The evidence concerns superpixel classification with fixed
 526   residual GCNs, complemented by hybrid GatedGCN rep-
 527   resentation studies and historical node experiments. The
 528   core hard-event and temporal tests use one teacher and previ-
 529   ously used validation or training graphs. Replication across
 530   teachers and tasks, direct One-Spike comparisons, and a
 531   modulewise decomposition of event boundaries and resid-
 532   ual paths remain open. Feature-dependent GatedGCN gates
 533   require a separate perturbation analysis, and changing in-
 534   put topology lies outside the experiments. Within these
 535   conditions, the reported message-fidelity and local-temporal-
 536   equality measures each leave information relevant to the final
 537   decision unspecified. The evaluation checklist makes that
 538   information visible without claiming a new converter or a
 539   conversion-wide error frequency.
